About HostelEase

Your Trusted On-Campus
Accommodation Partner

HostelEase manages student accommodation at two prime locations — Nadiad (near DDIT College) and Changa (opposite Charusat University). We provide a safe, supportive, and fully equipped living environment designed to help students focus on academics and personal growth.

Our hostels offer structured daily schedules, full-board meal packages, high-speed internet, and round-the-clock security — everything a university student needs under one roof.

  • 24/7 WiFi Connectivity
  • Full Meal Package
  • 24/7 Security & CCTV
  • Separate Boys & Girls Blocks
  • Warden Support
  • Medical Assistance
  • Laundry Facilities
  • Study Rooms

Facilities & Amenities

Everything Included

All rooms come with core facilities. No hidden charges, no surprise bills.

📶

High-Speed WiFi

Fiber internet across all floors, 24/7. Supports video calls, e-learning platforms, and research tools.

🍽️

Meal Service

Full-board meals — breakfast, lunch, and dinner — prepared in a hygienic, FSSAI-compliant kitchen.

🔒

24/7 Security

CCTV surveillance on all floors and entry points. Trained security staff present round the clock.

🧺

Laundry

Washing machines available on each floor. Separate facilities for boys and girls.

📚

Study Rooms

Dedicated quiet study rooms with proper lighting, ergonomic seating, and whiteboards.

⚕️

Medical Aid

First-aid station at warden's office. On-call medical professional available for emergencies.

🅿️

Parking

Secure covered parking for bicycles and two-wheelers within the hostel compound.

🧹

Housekeeping

Daily room cleaning and common area maintenance by trained housekeeping staff.
